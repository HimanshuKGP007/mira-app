Mira · product overview

One recording. One sound-by-sound report.

A child looks at a picture, says the word out loud, and Mira tells you which individual sounds in that word were said correctly — not just whether the whole word sounded roughly right.

How a single turn works

01
🖼️

Child sees a word

A picture and a prompt word appear — e.g. "sun". No typing, no reading required.

→
02
🎙️

Child says it

The app records a short voice clip through the microphone, right in the browser.

→
03
🧠

Mira listens

The recording is checked for a clean signal, then measured sound by sound against the word that was asked for.

→
04
🔍

Each sound is scored

Was every sound in the word produced correctly? If not, what came out instead?

→
05
📊

Result, instantly

Roughly a tenth of a second later, the child sees a fun result — and the data feeds two other views.

What "sound by sound" actually means

Take the word "sun". It's made of three sounds: s · u · n. Most tools would just tell you "80% correct." Mira tells you exactly which of the three was the problem:

"sun"
s — correct u — said as "a" n — correct

The same result, three ways

Kid view

A game, not a test

  • Progress trail with unlockable animal friends
  • Instant, encouraging feedback after every word
  • Next word is chosen adaptively, targeting the sound they need the most practice on
Parent view

Plain-language progress

  • How practice is trending over time
  • Which sound needs the most work right now
  • A short, auto-written summary of the pattern — no jargon
Clinician view

The full data, clinically framed

  • Every sound, every attempt, with a confidence figure
  • Error patterns grouped the way a speech-language pathologist thinks about them
  • A report they can read and sign off on

Where Mira draws the line

🛑

Mira measures. It never diagnoses. The output is always "this sound was/wasn't produced correctly" — never a severity score, a condition name, or a therapy recommendation. That judgment stays with the clinician. It's a rule built into the system itself, not just a design choice we could quietly walk back.
