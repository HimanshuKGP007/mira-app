## Therapist supply
- **[BLS Occupational Outlook: Speech-Language Pathologists](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/speech-language-pathologists.htm)**
  US count: 193,400 in 2025, 225,500 projected for 2035.
- **[Nair and Karuppali (2023), CoDAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10449094/)**
  India: 2,866 registered audiologists and SLPs as of February 2022. Authors say the real number may be higher.
- **[Hearing Health Matters: Status of Audiology in India (2017)](https://hearinghealthmatters.org/hearing-international/2017/status-audiology-india/)**
  Older India figure: 2,500 registered audiologists and SLPs. Describes a severe shortage.

## Relapse after therapy
- **[Craig and Hancock (1995), Australian Journal of Human Communication Disorders](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.3109/asl2.1995.23.issue-1.04)**
  Adults who stutter: 70% felt they relapsed. Most also felt they regained fluency later. Self-report only.
- **[Craig et al. (1998), Journal of Fluency Disorders](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0094730X97000284)**
  77 kids aged 9 to 14 who stutter. 3 in 10 relapsed at 12 months. Anxiety after treatment predicted relapse.
- **[ResearchGate: Recovery and relapse in stuttering, preliminary study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/269740952_Factors_related_to_Recovery_and_Relapse_in_Persons_with_Stuttering_Following_Treatment_A_Preliminary_Study)**
  Cites relapse rates up to 73% for stuttering.
- **Note:** No source was found for "60% of speech therapy patients relapse." The 40 to 60% figure online is for addiction treatment.

## Kids' voices
- **[Lee, Potamianos and Narayanan (1999), JASA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10089598/)**
  Kids' pitch and formants change with age. Adult levels around age 15. Male and female patterns split from age 11. ([Full PDF](https://sail.usc.edu/publications/files/LeeJASA1999.pdf))
- **[Potamianos: Review of children's speech acoustics](https://slp-ntua.github.io/potam/preprints/conf/2007_mmsp_children.pdf)**
  Kids' formants can be up to 50% higher than adult male values. Explains why adult speech models fail on kids.
- **Crowe and McLeod (2020), AJSLP 29(4):2155 to 2169** (cited in the notebook)
  Age by which kids learn each English consonant. 18,907 kids, 15 studies. US English norms.

## Regulation
- **[CDSCO: Guidance on Medical Device Software under MDR 2017 (CDSCO/MD/GD/MDSW/01/2026)](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/export/sites/CDSCO_WEB/Pdf-documents/Guidance-document-on-Medical-Device-Software-under-MDR-2017.pdf)**
  Defines "general wellness software." Wellness claims must not refer to diseases or disorders. Screening or monitoring a disorder makes software a medical device. Key for Mira's wellness plan.
- **[Emergo by UL: summary of the CDSCO guidance](https://www.emergobyul.com/news/india-cdsco-finalizes-guidance-medical-device-software)**
  Short summary of what counts as medical device software in India.

## Home practice
- **[Parents' experiences of home practice for speech sound disorders](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/331171498_Parents'_Experiences_of_Completing_Home_Practice_for_Speech_Sound_Disorders)**
  Interviews with parents about why home practice is hard. Supports the problem section.
- **[How SLTs and parents work together (PMC11606383)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11606383/)**
  Speech sound disorder prevalence ranges from 2.3% to 24.6% by definition and age. Useful to check the 10% TAM number.

## Datasets (from the notebook)
- **Svarah** (`ai4bharat/Svarah`): 6,607 recordings of fluent Indian English adults. Commercial use allowed.
- **speechocean762** (OpenSLR 101): the only labelled training data. 5,000 recordings, Mandarin-speaking learners.
- **UltraSuite:** 58 typical kids and about 65 kids with speech sound disorders. Closest public match to Mira's users. Non-commercial, British.
- **NNCES:** Indian kids aged 8 to 12, Telugu first language. Not yet acquired.
